## 📊 Projects

### 🇮🇳 Amazon India — Business Performance Dashboard
**Tools:** Excel · KPI design · Data cleaning
**Data:** 10,000 orders · Jan 2024 – Aug 2026 · 5 categories · 10 states

**Problem:** Give management one view of sales, profit, product mix, revenue lost to returns and cancellations, and where the business comes from.

**Headline numbers:** ₹15.58 crore sales · ₹3.32 crore profit · 21.3% margin · ₹15,579 average order value

**Key insights**
- **Electronics & Mobiles drives 79% of sales from just 30% of orders.** The top 5 products by sales are all electronics, so revenue is heavily concentrated in one category.
- **Almost 1 in 10 orders is lost.** 485 were returned and 500 cancelled (9.85% combined). Electronics has the highest return rate (5.4%), and Apparel the highest cancellation rate (5.7%).
- **Three states account for 49% of sales:** Maharashtra, Karnataka and Delhi.
- **Half of sales are paid by UPI (50%).** Card orders have the highest return and cancellation rate (11.0%), against 7.6% for Amazon Pay Later.
- **69% of sales are fulfilled by Amazon (FBA).**
- **Discounts did not hurt margin.** Discounted orders kept a 21.1–21.6% margin, compared with 20.8% for full-price orders.
- **Growth has been slow but is picking up.** Sales were flat from 2024 to 2025 (+0.7%), but Jan–Aug 2026 is up about 7.5% on the same period last year, with July 2026 the best month (₹64.4 lakh).

**Recommendations:** Reduce returns in Electronics through better product information and quality checks. Look into why Apparel orders get cancelled so often. Reduce dependence on a single category by growing Home & Kitchen, the second-largest at 11.5% of sales.

### 🛒 Supermart Grocery Sales Analytics
**Tools:** Excel · Data cleaning · Interactive dashboard (Year / Region / Category filters)
**Data:** 9,994 orders · 2015–2018 · 7 categories · 21 sub-categories · 23 cities

**Problem:** What drives sales and profit, and do discounts actually hurt profitability?

**Headline numbers:** ₹1.50 crore sales · ₹37.5 lakh profit · 25.1% margin · 22.7% average discount

**Key insights**
- **Sales grew every year, and growth is speeding up:** +5.3% in 2016, +23.6% in 2017, +28.6% in 2018.
- **Sales peak in the festive months.** September and November are the strongest months every year, and September 2018 was the best month overall (₹5.9 lakh).
- **No single category dominates.** The seven categories each hold 13.6–15.2% of sales, so growth has to come from the whole range.
- **West is the largest region (32% of sales)** and South the smallest (16%), though South has the best margin (25.6%).
- **Discounts showed no link to profit.** The correlation was close to zero (0.008), and margins stayed at 24.8–25.7% across all discount bands.
- **Some high-sales items have slightly weaker margins**, including Spices (23.8%), Masalas (24.2%) and Cakes (24.6%), though margins across the range are tightly clustered.

**Recommendations:** Run festive-season campaigns in September and November. Review pricing on high-sales, low-margin sub-categories like Spices and Masalas. Use the room that flat discount-to-profit results show, and test whether selective discounts can lift volume without costing margin.

**Data notes:** Handled two mixed date formats and documented every assumption. Treated Sub-Category as the "product" level, since the dataset has no SKU field.
